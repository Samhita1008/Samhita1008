## What I do for clients

- **Lead generation and outreach:** find prospects, draft personalized emails, and queue them for your approval before anything sends.
- **Inbox and support triage:** classify incoming messages, draft replies, and alert you on Telegram when a human is needed.
- **Content repurposing:** turn one video into posts, summaries and notes, organized in Notion.
- **Bookings and operations:** intake, confirmations, status tracking and logging in Google Sheets.
- **Document review workflows:** clause-by-clause contract review against a playbook, risk scoring, and routing to a human approver.

## How I work

- **Nothing sends without your approval.** Every outbound message goes through a human review step.
- **No black boxes.** Each system ships with a README, a pipeline diagram and engineering notes on what broke and how it was fixed.
- **Honest scoping.** Demo components are labeled as demos, and I don't quote results I haven't measured.
- **Built to transfer.** Your own API keys, one config node per workflow and plain-English node names, so nothing is locked to me.
- **Public workflows.** Several builds are on GitHub as full workflow files, so you can read how I work before you hire me.
